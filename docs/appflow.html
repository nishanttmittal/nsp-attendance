2. Owner's daily routine (1 minute)

  • Telegram tells you if something needs attention.
  • Problems tab (red count): missed punch → OK full day / Half day / real time · ⚡ unusually high OT → check before salary · absent a full month → Resign (password) / Keep · late 3+ & short-day lists are view-only (machine already cuts pay).
  • Floor: who's in, department strength, dinner count.
  • Anytime: Give advance (you or manager) — date, amount, cash/bank, remark → logged in the person's ledger + Telegram alert.

4. Anytime tools

  • Reports (📄 button or Telegram bot "report may performance"): Performance, Monthly Summary, In-Out, Missed Punches, OT, Late, Absent — any month, everyone / one typed name → file lands on Telegram.
  • Payslip: any person, any day (till yesterday) — PDF, Telegram, or 🟢 WhatsApp direct to the worker's number.
  • Salary register PDF → phone share sheet → WhatsApp.
  • Person page (tap any name): rate, phone, joining date, present % · late × · OT, advance ledger, increment history, every salary payment ever made, resign (password-protected).
  • ⚙ Settings: managers, action password, backup / restore.

5. Safety net

  • Salary formula verified against April payroll to the rupee (45/45 staff).
  • Missed punch never costs a worker a day; phantom-OT alarm; rules-change alarm at 6:45 AM.
  • Tick freezes figures — what you approve is exactly what gets paid.
  • Every advance, increment and payment is logged with date, remark and who did it.
  • Cloud data + scheduled auto-backup + one-tap manual backup.
Salary rules in one line: Base = salary ÷ days-in-month × (days − absent) · OT paid at salary ÷ days ÷ shift-hours (net of late/early) · full month with zero absence = +1 day · deductions: fine → loan → full outstanding advance (editable) · net never below ₹0, balance carries forward.
